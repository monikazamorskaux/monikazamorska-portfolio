About me

I've been working in the IT industry for over 8 years, and in 2022 I consciously chose UX as my career path. Since then, I've been continuously developing my skills, broadening my toolkit, and learning how to design in changing product environments.

For me, UX design is about building a bridge between technology and people.

I enjoy working on the product as a whole — organizing processes, solving problems, and balancing company goals with real user needs. That balance is what makes design most interesting to me.

How I currently use AI in the design process

Together with a teammate, I'm actively shaping how AI is integrated into our design workflow. It's a current priority for us, and we keep adapting the process as the tools and possibilities continue to evolve.

01

Feature concept visualisation

We recreate design system components in Claude Code to quickly assemble early feature concepts and show clients possible directions before implementation.

02

Brand-based application theming

We use AI-supported workflows to apply client branding to app templates and generate early visual directions for review and approval.

03

Legacy module mapping

We map older, undesigned modules onto our current component system to speed up updates, redesigns and new feature work.
